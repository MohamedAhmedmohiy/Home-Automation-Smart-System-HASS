Hardware Slot Pairing & Device Setup

Assign each physical relay slot (1–4) to a named appliance with a category icon.

Figure 3.2: Category & Icon

The 4-Channel Terminal Mapping

The ESP32 controller exposes four relay outputs (S1–S4). Each one maps to a single device in the app.

▣ Slot Selection Matrix

Pick a free slot; occupied slots are disabled automatically.

◈ Category Icons & Labeling

Choose lamp, fan, TV, AC or socket and give the device a name.

Saving pushes the mapping to the controller and Firebase instantly.
